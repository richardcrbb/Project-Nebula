WIREGUARD

In this tutorial, you'll learn how to set up a WireGuard VPN server using Docker Compose, providing a secure and efficient way to access your network remotely. WireGuard is a modern VPN protocol known for its simplicity, performance, and ease of use. By leveraging Docker Compose, you'll automate the creation and management of containers, making it easier to deploy and scale your VPN server. Throughout this guide, I'll walk you through the process of configuring the WireGuard server and adding a specific number of clients in a straightforward manner. By the end of the tutorial, you'll have a fully functional VPN server to securely connect from anywhere, whether it's your home, office, or on the go.
